03 · WHEN YOUR SUPPLIER IS HIT

Their incident, your deadlines

Your clock starts when you become aware of an incident that is significant for you — not when your supplier finishes investigating. If it hits your services, you report your own incident, even though the cause sits with the supplier.

    04 · WHO TO CALL
